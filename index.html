How a trade works

Four steps. The platform never touches gourdes.

Built to stop the usual scams

The rules are enforced by the database, not just by the screen.

🔒Locked firstSell ads hold the USDT up front. A seller cannot take the gourdes and disappear.
⏱️Timers, never auto-releaseUnpaid orders refund. A silent seller opens a dispute. Money is never released by a timer.
⚖️Real disputesStaff read the proof. Big orders need two agents. Fake proof means a ban.
🪪ID and name matchPayment accounts must be in the same name as the ID. Third-party payments are blocked.
📈Limits that grow with trustNew accounts start small. Limits rise with tier and clean history.
🧾Full audit trailEvery state change is logged in an append-only table. Illegal moves are blocked by the database.
