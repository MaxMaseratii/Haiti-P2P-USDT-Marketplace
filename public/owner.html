Haiti P2P · owner only

Owner tools

Put the lock contract on Arbitrum with your own wallet, take out your fees, pause, and decide disputes on-chain. Nothing here can touch users' money except what the contract allows. This page never sends your keys anywhere.

1Pick the network

2Connect your Arbitrum wallet

MetaMask, Rabby, Coinbase Wallet or any browser wallet. This wallet becomes the owner of the contract (it can pause it and change roles).

3Choose the wallets

••••••••••••••••••••••••
Save this secret key now. It is shown only on this page and is lost if you reload. Anyone with it can run the app wallet (lock and send), but it can never take users' money or your fees.
Use a different wallet to trade on the app yourself. The fee wallet is kept for fees only.

4Put the contract on the network

Your wallet asks you to confirm. It costs a few cents of ETH.

4bPractice only: give test USDT to a wallet

The seller wallet you trade with needs test USDT. Use a different wallet than the fee wallet (for example a second account in MetaMask).

5Give the app wallet a little ETH

The app wallet pays the network cost for locks, unlocks and gasless withdraws. On Arbitrum each one costs about 1 to 5 cents. Start with about $10 of ETH and top it up when it runs low.

6Copy these lines into Render

Render → your haiti-p2p service → Environment → add each line. Then save. The app restarts in real mode.

Deploy first (step 4).

After launch

★Your contract

$Take out your fees

Connect the fee wallet first. Fees wait inside the contract until you take them out.

⚖Decide a dispute on-chain

Use this only when the app says a decision is waiting for the arbiter. Connect the arbiter wallet. Paste the order number from the staff page.

⏸Emergency pause

Pause stops new deposits, ad holds and locks. Withdraws, unlocks and refunds still work, so nobody's money gets stuck. Owner wallet only.
